# Bab 3: Domain EDM (Evaluate, Direct and Monitor) — Objektif Tata Kelola (EDM01–EDM05)

> **Fokus Area:** *Governance of Enterprise I&T* (Dewan Komisaris, Dewan Direksi, Executive Committee, dan I&T Governance Board).
> Domain **EDM** memastikan bahwa seluruh kebutuhan pemangku kepentingan dievaluasi (**Evaluate**) untuk menentukan arah strategis melalui prioritisasi dan pengambilan keputusan (**Direct**), serta kinerja dan kepatuhan dipantau secara independen terhadap arah dan tujuan yang telah disepakati (**Monitor**).

---

## EDM01 — Ensured Governance Framework Setting and Maintenance

- **Area:** Governance | **Domain:** Evaluate, Direct and Monitor
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 29–34
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Analyze and articulate the requirements for the governance of enterprise I&T. Put in place and maintain governance components with clarity of authority and responsibilities to achieve the enterprise's mission, goals and objectives.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Provide a consistent approach integrated and aligned with the enterprise governance approach. I&T-related decisions are made in line with the enterprise's strategies and objectives and desired value is realized. To that end, ensure that I&T-related processes are overseen effectively and transparently; compliance with legal, contractual and regulatory requirements is confirmed; and the governance requirements for board members are met.

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — EDM01

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Evaluasi, pengarahan, dan pemantauan kerangka kerja tata kelola TI (IT Governance Charter, pendelegasian wewenang Direksi/Komisaris, dan struktur komite pengarah TI).
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Pedoman Tata Kelola TI (IT Governance Charter / Board Charter) dan SK Komite Pengarah TI (IT Steering Committee).
  1. Matriks Pendelegasian Wewenang (Delegation of Authority / DoA) untuk keputusan investasi, arsitektur, dan risiko TI.
  1. Notulensi rapat Dewan Komisaris/Direksi dan Komite Pengarah TI selama 12 bulan terakhir.
  1. Laporan pemantauan efektivitas tata kelola TI dan tindak lanjut temuan regulasi (POJK/SPBE/UU PDP).
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Periksa apakah IT Governance Charter telah disahkan oleh Dewan Direksi/Komisaris dan menetapkan batas wewenang (threshold) keputusan TI secara tegas.
  - ToE: Uji sampel keputusan strategis TI tahun berjalan dan pastikan telah disetujui sesuai matriks otorisasi serta dievaluasi kinerjanya oleh Board.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Komite Pengarah TI (ITSC) hanya terbentuk di atas kertas namun tidak pernah rapat atau tidak dihadiri eksekutif bisnis.
  - ⚠️ Keputusan investasi TI bernilai besar diambil secara sepihak oleh fungsi TI tanpa persetujuan Board/Executive Committee.

## EDM02 — Ensured Benefits Delivery

- **Area:** Governance | **Domain:** Evaluate, Direct and Monitor
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 35–40
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Optimize the value to the business from investments in business processes, I&T services and I&T assets.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Secure optimal value from I&T-enabled initiatives, services and assets; cost-efficient delivery of solutions and services; and a reliable and accurate picture of costs and likely benefits so that business needs are supported effectively and efficiently.

### 2. Komponen A: Praktik Proses, Metrik, Aktivitas & Level Kapabilitas (EDM02)

#### EDM02.01 — Establish the target investment mix

> **Deskripsi Praktik:** Review and ensure clarity of the enterprise and I&T strategies and current services. Define an appropriate investment mix based on cost, alignment with strategy, type of benefit for the programs in the portfolio, degree of risk, and financial measures such as cost and expected return on investment (ROI) over the full economic life cycle. Adjust the enterprise and I&T strategies where necessary.

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**
- a. Percent of I&T investments traceable to enterprise strategy
- b. Percent of I&T investments based on cost, alignment with strategy, financial measures (e.g., cost and ROI over the full economic life cycle), degree of risk and type of benefit for the programs in the portfolio

**Daftar Lengkap Aktivitas & Target Level Kapabilitas (CMMI 2–5):**

| No. | Aktivitas Praktik Resmi COBIT 2019 | Capability Level |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | 1. Create and maintain portfolios of I&T-enabled investment programs, IT services and IT assets, which form the basis for the current IT budget and support the I&T tactical and strategic plans. | **Level 2** |
| 2 | 2. Obtain a common understanding between IT and the other business functions on the potential opportunities for IT to enable and contribute to enterprise strategy. | **Level 2** |
| 3 | 3. Identify the broad categories of information systems, applications, data, IT services, infrastructure, I&T assets, resources, skills, practices, controls and relationships needed to support the enterprise strategy. | **Level 2** |
| 4 | 4. Agree on I&T goals, taking into account the interrelationships between the enterprise strategy and the I&T services, assets and other resources. Identify and leverage synergies that can be achieved. | **Level 2** |
| 5 | 5. Define an investment mix that achieves the right balance among a number of dimensions, including an appropriate balance of short- and long-term returns, financial and nonfinancial benefits, and high- and low-risk investments. | **Level 3** |

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — EDM02

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — EDM05

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Transparansi pelaporan kinerja, risiko, dan kepatuhan TI kepada pemangku kepentingan internal maupun regulator eksternal.
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Kebijakan Komunikasi dan Pelaporan Pemangku Kepentingan TI (Stakeholder Communication & Reporting Policy).
  1. Paket laporan berkala TI untuk Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Pemegang Saham, dan Regulator (OJK, BI, BSSN, Kominfo).
  1. Bukti verifikasi/validasi akurasi metrik kinerja TI sebelum dilaporkan ke Board/Regulator.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Pastikan kebutuhan pelaporan seluruh stakeholder (termasuk regulator) telah diidentifikasi dengan jadwal dan format standar.
  - ToE: Telusuri angka SLA, insiden, dan status proyek pada laporan Board kembali ke data sumber (ITSM/PMO) untuk menguji keakuratan dan bebas dari manipulasi (watermelon effect).
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ 'Watermelon Reporting' (hijau di luar, merah di dalam): laporan ke Direksi selalu tampak hijau/normal padahal pengguna mengalami gangguan parah.
  - ⚠️ Keterlambatan pelaporan insiden siber/kebocoran data ke regulator melampaui tenggat waktu wajib (mis. 3x24 jam UU PDP).
