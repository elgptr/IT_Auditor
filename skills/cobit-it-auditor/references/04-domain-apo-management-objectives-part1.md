## APO01 — Managed I&T Management Framework

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Align, Plan and Organize
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 55–64
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Design the management system for enterprise I&T based on enterprise goals and other design factors. Based on this design, implement all required components of the management system.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Implement a consistent management approach for enterprise governance requirements to be met, covering governance components such as management processes; organizational structures; roles and responsibilities; reliable and repeatable activities; information items; policies and procedures; skills and competencies; culture and behavior; and services, infrastructure and applications

### 2. Komponen A: Praktik Proses, Metrik, Aktivitas & Level Kapabilitas (APO01)

#### APO01.01 — Design the management system for enterprise I&T

> **Deskripsi Praktik:** Design a management system tailored to the needs of the enterprise. Management needs of the enterprise are defined through the use of the goals cascade and by application of design factors. Ensure the governance components are integrated and aligned with the enterprise’s governance and management philosophy and operating style.

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**
- a. Number of formal sign-offs by applicable governance structures of the priority objectives for the I&T management system
- b. Percent of governance components integrated and aligned with the enterprise’s governance and management philosophy and operating style

**Daftar Lengkap Aktivitas & Target Level Kapabilitas (CMMI 2–5):**

| No. | Aktivitas Praktik Resmi COBIT 2019 | Capability Level |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | 1. Obtain an understanding of the enterprise vision, direction and strategy as well as the current enterprise context and challenges. | **Level 2** |
| 2 | 2. Consider the enterprise’s internal environment, including management culture and philosophy, risk tolerance, security and privacy policy, ethical values, code of conduct, accountability, and requirements for management integrity. | **Level 2** |
| 3 | 3. Apply the COBIT goals cascade and design factors to the enterprise strategy and context to decide on priorities for the management system and, thus, for implementation of management objective priorities. | **Level 2** |
| 4 | 4. Validate selected priorities for implementation of management objectives with industry-specific good practices or requirements (e.g., industry-specific regulations) and with appropriate governance structures. | **Level 3** |

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — APO01

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Perancangan sistem manajemen TI (I&T Management System), struktur organisasi TI, kebijakan inti TI, dan peningkatan berkelanjutan.
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Bagan Struktur Organisasi TI, Job Description, dan Matriks RACI seluruh fungsi TI.
  1. Hierarki Kebijakan, Standar, dan Prosedur Operasional Standar (SOP) TI serta bukti tinjauan tahunan.
  1. Dokumentasi pemetaan COBIT Design Factors dan target kapabilitas proses TI.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Periksa kelengkapan struktur organisasi TI, pemisahan tugas (Segregation of Duties / SoD), dan cakupan kebijakan TI.
  - ToE: Uji apakah kebijakan TI dikomunikasikan kepada seluruh pegawai, dipahami, dan ditinjau ulang minimal setahun sekali.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Perangkapan jabatan kritis (mis. Head Development merangkap Head IT Operations atau CIO merangkap CISO/Audit).
  - ⚠️ Kebijakan TI sudah usang (>3 tahun tidak diperbarui) dan tidak mencerminkan arsitektur cloud/kerja jarak jauh saat ini.

## APO02 — Managed Strategy

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Align, Plan and Organize
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 65–72
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Provide a holistic view of the current business and I&T environment, the future direction, and the initiatives required to migrate to the desired future environment. Ensure that the desired level of digitization is integral to the future direction and the I&T strategy. Assess the organization’s current digital maturity and develop a road map to close the gaps. With the business, rethink internal operations as well as customer-facing activities. Ensure focus on the transformation journey across the organization. Leverage enterprise architecture building blocks, governance components and the organization's ecosystem, including externally provided services and related capabilities, to enable reliable but agile and efficient response to strategic objectives.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Support the digital transformation strategy of the organization and deliver the desired value through a road map of incremental changes. Use a holistic I&T approach, ensuring that each initiative is clearly connected to an overarching strategy. Enable change in all different aspects of the organization, from channels and processes to data, culture, skills, operating model and incentives.

## APO03 — Managed Enterprise Architecture

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Align, Plan and Organize
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 73–80
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Establish a common architecture consisting of business process, information, data, application and technology architecture layers. ### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — APO07

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Pengelolaan SDM TI: rekrutmen, retensi, matriks kompetensi (SFIA/e-CF), evaluasi kinerja, suksesi, dan pengelolaan tenaga kontrak/outsourcing.
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Peta Kompetensi SDM TI (Skills Matrix), rencana pelatihan tahunan, dan catatan jam pelatihan per pegawai.
  1. Prosedur Background Check, Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), serta proses onboarding/offboarding pegawai dan kontraktor TI.
  1. Rencana suksesi (Succession Plan) untuk posisi kritis TI dan hasil evaluasi kinerja SDM/kontraktor.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Periksa kebijakan pengelolaan SDM TI termasuk persyaratan background screening dan pencabutan akses saat mutasi/resign.
  - ToE: Ambil sampel pegawai/kontraktor baru dan yang berhenti dalam 12 bulan terakhir; uji kelengkapan background check, NDA, serta ketepatan waktu pencabutan hak akses (<24 jam saat terminasi).
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Akun milik mantan karyawan atau kontraktor vendor masih aktif di sistem produksi berbulan-bulan setelah kontrak berakhir.
  - ⚠️ Tingkat turnover SDM keamanan/pengembang sangat tinggi tanpa adanya transfer pengetahuan (knowledge transfer) dan rencana suksesi.
