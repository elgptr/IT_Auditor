# Bab 5: Domain APO Bagian 2 (Align, Plan and Organize) — Objektif Manajemen (APO08–APO14)

> **Fokus Area:** *Management of Enterprise I&T — Relationships, Service Agreements, Vendors, Quality, Risk, Security (ISMS) & Data Governance*.
> Bagian kedua domain **APO** mencakup pengelolaan hubungan bisnis-TI (`APO08`), perjanjian tingkat layanan/SLA (`APO09`), manajemen vendor/pihak ketiga (`APO10`), manajemen mutu (`APO11`), manajemen risiko operasional TI (`APO12`), sistem manajemen keamanan informasi/ISMS (`APO13`), dan tata kelola data (`APO14`).

## APO08 — Managed Relationships

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Align, Plan and Organize
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 107–112
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Manage relationships with business stakeholders in a formalized and transparent way that ensures mutual trust and a combined focus on achieving the strategic goals within the constraints of budgets and risk tolerance. Base relationships on open and transparent communication, a common language, and the willingness to take ownership and accountability for key decisions on both sides. Business and IT must work together to create successful enterprise outcomes in support of the enterprise objectives.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Enable the right knowledge, skills and behaviors to create improved outcomes, increased confidence, mutual trust and effective use of resources that stimulate a productive relationship with business stakeholders.

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — APO08

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Pengelolaan hubungan strategis dan operasional antara fungsi TI dan unit bisnis (Business-IT Relationship Management).
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Piagam/Prosedur Business Relationship Management (BRM) dan penunjukan Relationship Manager untuk tiap unit bisnis.
  1. Notulensi rapat koordinasi rutin antara BRM/TI dengan Business Process Owners.
  1. Hasil survei kepuasan unit bisnis terhadap layanan dan responsivitas TI beserta rencana tindak lanjutnya.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Evaluasi apakah peran Relationship Manager telah ditetapkan secara formal untuk menjembatani kebutuhan bisnis dan solusi TI.
  - ToE: Tinjau bukti kolaborasi bisnis-TI dalam perumusan kebutuhan baru dan tindak lanjut keluhan/ketidakpuasan unit bisnis.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Hubungan bisnis dan TI bersifat transaksional/konfrontatif (saling menyalahkan), memicu maraknya Shadow IT (unit bisnis membeli SaaS sendiri tanpa sepengetahuan TI).
  - ⚠️ Survei kepuasan pengguna dilakukan namun hasilnya tidak pernah dianalisis atau ditindaklanjuti.

## APO09 — Managed Service Agreements

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Align, Plan and Organize
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 113–118
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Align I&T-enabled products and services and service levels with enterprise needs and expectations, including identification, specification, design, publishing, agreement, and monitoring of I&T products and services, service levels and performance indicators.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Ensure that I&T products, services and service levels meet current and future enterprise needs.

### 2. Komponen A: Praktik Proses, Metrik, Aktivitas & Level Kapabilitas (APO09)

#### APO09.01 — Identify I&T services

> **Deskripsi Praktik:** Analyze business requirements and the degree to which I&T-enabled services and service levels support business processes. Discuss and agree with the business on potential services and service levels. Compare potential service levels against the current service portfolio; identify new or changed services or service level options.

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**
- a. Number of business activities that are not supported by any I&T service
- b. Number of obsolete services identified

**Daftar Lengkap Aktivitas & Target Level Kapabilitas (CMMI 2–5):**

| No. | Aktivitas Praktik Resmi COBIT 2019 | Capability Level |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | 1. Assess current I&T services and service levels to identify gaps between existing services and the business activities they support. Identify areas for improvement of existing services and service level options. | **Level 2** |
| 2 | 2. Analyze, study and estimate future demand and confirm capacity of existing I&T-enabled services. | **Level 2** |
| 3 | 3. Analyze business process activities to identify the need for new or redesigned I&T services. | **Level 3** |
| 4 | 4. Compare identified requirements to existing service components in the portfolio. If possible, package existing service components (I&T services, service level options and service packages) into new service packages to meet identified business requirements. | **Level 3** |
| 5 | 5. Regularly review the portfolio of I&T services with portfolio management and business relationship management to identify obsolete services. Agree on retirement and propose change. | **Level 3** |
| 6 | 6. Where possible, match demands to service packages and create standardized services to obtain overall efficiencies. | **Level 4** |

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — APO09

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Pengelolaan Katalog Layanan TI (Service Catalogue), Service Level Agreement (SLA), dan Operational Level Agreement (OLA).
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Katalog Layanan TI (IT Service Catalogue) yang aktif beserta definisi paket layanan dan pemilik layanan (Service Owner).
  1. Dokumen SLA antara TI dan unit bisnis serta OLA internal antar tim teknis TI.
  1. Laporan pemantauan pencapaian SLA bulanan, analisis pelanggaran SLA (SLA Breach), dan rencana perbaikan layanan.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Periksa apakah seluruh layanan produksi memiliki definisi layanan di Service Catalogue dan SLA yang disepakati bersama unit bisnis.
  - ToE: Uji sampel laporan SLA bulanan terhadap log insiden/gangguan aktual untuk memastikan metrik ketersediaan (uptime) dan waktu respons dihitung secara akurat.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ SLA ditetapkan secara sepihak oleh TI tanpa kesepakatan unit bisnis, atau target SLA tidak selaras dengan SLA kontrak vendor pendukung.
  - ⚠️ Layanan kritis produksi beroperasi tanpa SLA formal dan tanpa pemantauan ketersediaan.

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Pengelolaan Tata Kelola Data (Data Governance), siklus hidup data, kamus data/metadata, kualitas data, dan kepatuhan privasi/retensi data.
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Kebijakan Tata Kelola Data (Data Governance Policy), Skema Klasifikasi Data, dan Daftar Data Owner / Data Steward.
  1. Kamus Data Korporat (Business Glossary / Metadata Repository), Data Lineage, dan Standar Kualitas Data.
  1. Kebijakan Retensi, Pengarsipan, dan Pemusnahan Data (selaras dengan UU PDP / GDPR) beserta laporan audit kualitas data.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Periksa apakah seluruh aset data kritis dan data pribadi (PII) telah diklasifikasikan dan memiliki Data Owner serta aturan retensi.
  - ToE: Uji sampel tabel database produksi terhadap standar kualitas data (akurasi, kelengkapan, konsistensi, keunikan) dan verifikasi pelaksanaan masking/anonimisasi pada lingkungan non-produksi.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Salinan data produksi yang berisi data pribadi nasabah/pelanggan (PII) digunakan secara mentah (tanpa masking) di lingkungan Development/UAT.
  - ⚠️ Ketiadaan Data Owner yang jelas sehingga terjadi inkonsistensi laporan manajemen antar departemen (multiple versions of truth).
