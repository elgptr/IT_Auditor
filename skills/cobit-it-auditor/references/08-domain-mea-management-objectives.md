# Bab 8: Domain MEA (Monitor, Evaluate and Assess) — Objektif Manajemen (MEA01–MEA04)

> **Fokus Area:** *Management of Enterprise I&T — Performance Monitoring, System of Internal Control, External Compliance & Managed Assurance*.
> Domain **MEA** menangani pemantauan kinerja, penilaian kecukupan pengendalian internal (ITGC & ITAC), kepatuhan terhadap regulasi eksternal, serta penyelenggaraan audit/asurans independen.

---

## MEA01 — Managed Performance and Conformance Monitoring

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Monitor, Evaluate and Assess
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 273–278
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Collect, validate and evaluate enterprise and alignment goals and metrics. Monitor that processes and practices are performing against agreed performance and conformance goals and metrics. Provide reporting that is systematic and timely.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Provide transparency of performance and conformance and drive achievement of goals.

### 2. Komponen A: Praktik Proses, Metrik, Aktivitas & Level Kapabilitas (MEA01)

#### MEA01.01 — Establish a monitoring approach

> **Deskripsi Praktik:** Engage with stakeholders to establish and maintain a monitoring approach to define the objectives, scope and method for measuring business solution and service delivery and contribution to enterprise objectives. Integrate this approach with the corporate performance management system.

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**
- a. Percent of processes with defined goals and metrics
- b. Percent of integration of monitoring approach within corporate performance management system

**Daftar Lengkap Aktivitas & Target Level Kapabilitas (CMMI 2–5):**

| No. | Aktivitas Praktik Resmi COBIT 2019 | Capability Level |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |

| 2 | 2. Engage with stakeholders and communicate the enterprise requirements and objectives for monitoring, aggregating and reporting, using common definitions (e.g., business glossary, metadata and taxonomy), baselining and benchmarking. | **Level 2** |
| 3 | 3. Align and continually maintain the monitoring and evaluation approach with the enterprise approach and the tools to be used for data gathering and enterprise reporting (e.g., business intelligence applications). | **Level 2** |

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — MEA01

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Pemantauan dan evaluasi kinerja serta kesesuaian TI (Performance and Conformance Monitoring): pengumpulan metrik, Balanced Scorecard TI, dan tindakan korektif.
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Kerangka kerja pengukuran kinerja TI (KPI, KGI, SLA, dan metrik kapabilitas proses) beserta target tahunan.
  1. Dashboard dan laporan pemantauan kinerja dan kepatuhan TI periodik.
  1. Daftar rencana tindakan perbaikan (Corrective Action Plans) atas metrik kinerja yang berada di bawah target.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Periksa apakah metrik kinerja yang dipantau selaras dengan Goals Cascade (mendukung Alignment Goals dan Enterprise Goals).
  - ToE: Uji keandalan proses pengumpulan data metrik dan pastikan setiap deviasi kinerja yang signifikan ditindaklanjuti dengan tindakan korektif terukur.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Organisasi mengumpulkan ratusan metrik teknis namun tidak ada satu pun yang mengukur dampak terhadap layanan bisnis atau ditindaklanjuti saat merah.
  - ⚠️ Data kinerja dikompilasi secara manual tanpa validasi sumber sehingga rentan dimanipulasi.

## MEA02 — Managed System of Internal Control

- **Area:** Management | **Domain:** Monitor, Evaluate and Assess
- **Referensi Halaman Resmi (COBIT 2019 Framework):** hlm. 279–284
- **Deskripsi Resmi:** Continuously monitor and evaluate the control environment, including self-assessments and self-awareness. Enable management to identify control deficiencies and inefficiencies and to initiate improvement actions. Plan, organize and maintain standards for internal control assessment and process control effectiveness.
- **Tujuan (Purpose Statement):** Obtain transparency for key stakeholders on the adequacy of the system of internal controls and thus provide trust in operations, confidence in the achievement of enterprise objectives and an adequate understanding of residual risk.

### 1. Pemetaan Goals Cascade & Metrik Tujuan (MEA02)

| Kategori Tujuan | Kode | Nama Tujuan Resmi | Dimensi BSC | Hubungan | Contoh Metrik Indikator (KPI / KGI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Enterprise Goal** | `EG03` | Compliance with external laws and regulations | Financial | **P** | • Cost of regulatory noncompliance, including settlements and fines<br>• Number of regulatory noncompliance issues causing public comment or negative publicity |
| **Enterprise Goal** | `EG11` | Compliance with internal policies | Internal | **P** | • Number of incidents related to noncompliance with policy<br>• Percent of stakeholders who understand policies |
| **Alignment Goal** | `AG01` | I&T compliance and support for business compliance with external laws and regulations | Financial | **S** | • Cost of IT noncompliance, including settlements and fines, and the impact of reputational loss |
| **Alignment Goal** | `AG02` | Managed I&T-related risk | Financial | **S** | • Frequency of update of risk profile |
| **Alignment Goal** | `AG04` | Quality of technology-related financial information | Financial | **S** | • Transparency of I&T costs, benefits and risk |
| **Alignment Goal** | `AG05` | Delivery of I&T services in line with business requirements | Customer | **S** | • Number of business disruptions due to IT service incidents |
| **Alignment Goal** | `AG07` | Security of information, processing infrastructure and applications, and privacy | Internal | **S** | • Number of confidentiality incidents causing financial loss, business disruption or public embarrassment |
| **Alignment Goal** | `AG09` | Delivering programs on time, on budget and meeting requirements and quality standards | Internal | **S** | • Number of programs/projects on time and within budget |

### 2. Komponen A: Praktik Proses, Metrik, Aktivitas & Level Kapabilitas (MEA02)

#### MEA02.01 — Monitor internal controls

> **Deskripsi Praktik:** Continuously monitor, benchmark and improve the I&T control environment and control framework to meet organizational objectives.

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**
- a. Number of major internal control breaches
- b. Percent of controls environment and framework continuously monitored, benchmarked and improved to meet organizational objectives

**Daftar Lengkap Aktivitas & Target Level Kapabilitas (CMMI 2–5):**

| No. | Aktivitas Praktik Resmi COBIT 2019 | Capability Level |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | 1. Identify the boundaries of the internal control system. For example, consider how organizational internal controls take into account outsourced and/or offshore development or production activities. | **Level 3** |
| 2 | 2. Assess the status of external service providers’ internal controls. Confirm that service providers comply with legal and regulatory requirements and contractual obligations. | **Level 3** |
| 3 | 3. Perform internal control monitoring and evaluation activities based on organizational governance standards and industry-accepted frameworks and practices. Also include monitoring and evaluation of the efficiency and effectiveness of managerial supervisory activities. | **Level 3** |
| 4 | 4. Ensure that control exceptions are promptly reported, followed up and analyzed, and appropriate corrective actions are prioritized and implemented according to the risk management profile (e.g., classify certain exceptions as a key risk and others as a non-key risk). | **Level 3** |
| 5 | 5. Consider independent evaluations of the internal control system (e.g., by internal audit or peers). | **Level 3** |
| 6 | 6. Maintain the internal control system, considering ongoing changes in business and I&T risk, the organizational control environment, and relevant business and I&T processes. If gaps exist, evaluate and recommend changes. | **Level 4** |
| 7 | 7. Regularly evaluate the performance of the control framework, benchmarking against industry accepted standards and good practices. Consider formal adoption of a continuous improvement approach to internal control monitoring. | **Level 5** |

This evidence assures the enterprise that controls meet requirements related to business, regulatory and social responsibilities.

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**
- a. Number of weaknesses identified by external qualification and certification reports
- b. Number of controls being monitored and tested to ensure that controls within business processes operate effectively

**Contoh Metrik Praktik (Example Metrics):**

### 5. Program Kerja Audit TI (Kertas Kerja Audit / KKA) — MEA04

- **Fokus Pengujian Audit:** Penyediaan Asurans TI Independen (Managed Assurance): perencanaan audit TI berbasis risiko, independensi auditor, pelaksanaan audit, dan pemantauan tindak lanjut temuan audit.
- **Daftar Permintaan Dokumen Bukti (PBC — *Provided by Client*):**
  1. Piagam Audit Internal (Internal Audit Charter) dan Rencana Audit TI Tahunan Berbasis Risiko (Risk-Based IT Audit Plan).
  1. Kertas Kerja Audit (KKA / Audit Workpapers), Laporan Hasil Audit (LHA) TI, serta laporan asurans eksternal.
  1. Log Pemantauan Tindak Lanjut Temuan Audit (Audit Finding Follow-up Register) beserta bukti verifikasi penutupan temuan.
- **Prosedur Pengujian Kontrol (Test of Design / ToD & Test of Operating Effectiveness / ToE):**
  - ToD: Evaluasi independensi, objektivitas, dan kecukupan kompetensi (CISA/CISSP/COBIT) fungsi asurans/audit TI serta cakupan perencanaan audit berbasis risiko.
  - ToE: Periksa ketepatan waktu penyelesaian tindak lanjut temuan audit (Agreed Management Actions) dan pastikan temuan baru dinyatakan 'Closed' setelah diverifikasi buktinya oleh auditor.
- **Indikator Temuan / Red Flags Audit:**
  - ⚠️ Banyak temuan audit TI berisiko tinggi yang sudah melewati batas waktu komitmen (Overdue Findings) selama bertahun-tahun tanpa eskalasi ke Komite Audit.
  - ⚠️ Temuan audit ditutup hanya berdasarkan janji manajemen atau dokumen kebijakan draf tanpa verifikasi implementasi efektif di lapangan.
